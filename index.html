What is Restore?

Hey! We’re a small group of lifelong Fortnite fans who missed the early‑season vibe. Restore rebuilds the classic battle‑royale experience from Seasons 19 aka chapter 3 season 1. Everything you loved – the Battle Bus, the iconic Loot Lake, the legendary Tilted Towers – is back, but tuned for today’s hardware. All data is stored securely and respects Epic’s EULA. Jump in, have fun, and help shape the next chapters!

Features

Classic Maps

All original locations from Seasons 1‑5, faithfully rebuilt and tuned for modern hardware.

Original Weapons

Every classic gun, shotgun and sniper you loved – authentic stats, nostalgic feel.

Battle Bus Reimagined

Soar from the nostalgic Bus with a fresh launch system that feels smooth and responsive.

Community‑Driven

Vote on map additions, events, and updates – the community decides the roadmap.

Arena Late‑Game

Jump back into the legendary Arena mode, climb the ranks, and own the victory circle!

Daily Item Shop

Fresh daily offers, V‑bucks for kills & wins – just the way the OG shop felt.
