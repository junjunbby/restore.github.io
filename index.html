What is Restore?

Restore is a fan‑driven project that brings back the original Fortnite battle‑royale experience from its peak. Full maps, classic weapons,the iconic Battle Bus, Arena and so much more is all back, ready for you to jump in and relive the golden era.
We are a community‑first initiative; all data is stored securely and respects Epic’s EULA. Join us and help shape the future of the OG experience!
