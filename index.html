Frequently Asked Questions

Is Restore safe and legal?
Restore is a community-run project that allows players to experience Chapter 3 Season 1. While we respect Epic Games' intellectual property, this is a fan-made project for nostalgic purposes. Use at your own discretion.
How do I connect to the server?
Simply download the Restore Launcher, create an account, and click "Play". The launcher handles everything automatically - no manual configuration needed!
Can I use my Epic Games account?
No, Restore uses separate accounts to keep your Epic Games account safe. You'll create a new Restore account when you first launch the game.
What weapons and items are available?
We feature the complete Chapter 3 Season 1 loot pool including the MK-Seven Assault Rifle, Striker Pump Shotgun, Auto Shotgun, Ranger Assault Rifle, and all the iconic items from that season.
Are there any costs involved?
Restore is completely free to play! However, donations help us keep servers running and maintain quality. Donors receive optional perks like priority queue and exclusive cosmetics.
What game modes are available?
We offer Solo, Duo,plus competitive Arena matches. Creative mode and LTMs are planned for future updates!
